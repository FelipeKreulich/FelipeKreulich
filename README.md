## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a **Software Engineer** passionate about building **scalable, secure and high-performance** applications — from the database all the way to the interface.

- 💼 Full Stack Developer @ **POP Tecnologia e Mobilidade**
- 🏗️ I enjoy designing **clean architectures** and solving complex technical challenges
- ⚙️ Experience across **backend, distributed systems, APIs & cloud infrastructure**
- 📱 Building mobile apps with **Kotlin (Android)** and **SwiftUI**
- 🔐 Background in **cybersecurity & networking**
- ✍️ I write weekly on my [blog](https://kreulich-blog.vercel.app) about web dev, career & AI
- 🌍 Based in **Lisboa, Portugal** · open to **freelance & full-time**
